Meet Blaine

Blaine has been behind the chair for over a decade, and behind this chair for the part that matters. He learned the trade the old way — apprenticing under barbers who measured twice and cut once — and he runs the shop the same way: good conversation, no rush, and a cut you don't have to fix when you get home.

Classic taper or modern fade, full beard or first shave, Blaine's done it a few thousand times. Sit down, tell him what you're thinking, and he'll take it from there.
